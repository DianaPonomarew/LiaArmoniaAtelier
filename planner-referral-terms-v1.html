Referral Partnership
Terms & Conditions.

This agreement explains the complimentary studio showcase and success-based introductions arranged by LIA ARMONÍA, operated by Diana Ponomarew, Germany ("LIA"), and the independent studio identified in the Partner Profile ("Partner"). By expressly accepting the version linked in the Partner Profile, the authorized representative agrees to the terms below on behalf of the Partner.

Simple terms, shared success. No listing fee. No exclusivity. No guaranteed enquiries or bookings. A 15% commission becomes payable only on qualifying fees actually received from a client introduced by LIA.

1. Collection and independent services

LIA may present the Partner’s approved professional profile in its Wedding Planner Collection and may introduce prospective clients. LIA does not guarantee publication at a particular time, lead volume or bookings. Partner decides whether to accept a project, sets its own prices and service agreement, and performs all services independently. Neither party may bind the other to third-party commitments.

2. What counts as an introduction

A “LIA Introduction” is a prospective client whose enquiry is assigned a LIA referral reference and whose identity/contact details LIA shares directly with the Partner for wedding services, with the client’s permission. LIA will identify the prospective client and the referral reference when making the introduction. If the client was already in active direct discussions with the Partner about the same wedding before that introduction, the Partner must notify LIA in writing with relevant dated evidence within five business days. The parties will resolve any genuine prior-client dispute before treating it as commissionable.

3. Success-based referral fee

If a client introduced by LIA enters a contract with the Partner for its own wedding planning, coordination, production or creative services within 12 months of the introduction, the Partner owes LIA 15% of the net service fees actually received from that client under the resulting booking. “Net service fees” excludes VAT or sales tax, refunds, and amounts passed through to independent third-party suppliers, venues or vendors without markup. For an eligible booking, fees paid after the 12-month booking window remain commissionable, including installments. Commission does not arise merely because an enquiry or introduction takes place.

4. Booking notification and payment

The Partner will notify LIA in writing within five business days after a referred client signs a booking agreement, stating the LIA referral reference, contracted services and agreed service fee. The Partner will also report each client payment received, including amount and date, within five business days of receipt, and provide a reasonable fee breakdown if asked. For each payment received from an eligible booking, the corresponding 15% referral commission is payable to LIA within 14 calendar days after the Partner receives that client payment, against an invoice or agreed billing instructions. If a qualifying client payment is later refunded, the associated referral fee will be corrected or credited against the next fee.

5. Introductions and confidentiality

Direct studio contact details need not be published in the Collection. Both parties will treat non-public client details and confidential commercial information as confidential and use them only to evaluate and deliver the relevant wedding services, subject to applicable data protection laws. LIA will obtain the client's consent before transferring identifiable contact information to the Partner. A Partner may decline an individual introduction without penalty.

6. Duration and ending the partnership

These terms apply from the Partner’s express acceptance until either party ends the arrangement by written notice. Ending the partnership does not remove obligations for qualifying introductions already made before termination, including fees received afterward. Changes to the commission rate or material conditions apply only if separately agreed; LIA will not impose changed terms unilaterally on existing referrals.

7. Governing law and communication

The parties intend German law to govern this business-to-business arrangement, without excluding mandatory rules that apply in another jurisdiction. Formal notices and referral confirmations may be sent by email to the addresses supplied in the Partner Profile and LIA’s contact address below. Any individual statutory form or licensing requirement that applies to a particular jurisdiction takes precedence where mandatory.

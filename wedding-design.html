No venue yet?

You do not need to begin with one. If you are drawn to a Lia Armonía design concept, we can source and coordinate a real venue whose architecture, proportions, setting and atmosphere support that direction. From there, the concept is developed specifically around the selected venue, adapting the spatial design, scale, materials and visual language to the real setting. One direction. One suitable place. One cohesive celebration.

Private Launch Commissions

Ahead of the official Lia Armonía launch, three celebrations that feel especially aligned with the atelier are being selected for Private Launch Commissions. A private introductory commission rate may be offered after review; selection considers creative fit, venue, scope, timing and readiness.

THE ATELIER PHILOSOPHY

Connected
by design.

LIA ARMONÍA is an international wedding design atelier built around architecture, atmosphere and a deeply personal point of view. We imagine distinctive design concepts and connect couples, venues, planners and creative teams around the world.

Rather than imposing one recognizable look on every celebration, we consider the light, proportions, materials and character of each setting. From the first sketch to a shared visual direction, we aim for a celebration that feels unmistakably its own.

Meet the founder. Diana Ponomarew brings an international business and project management background together with design, visual innovation and academic work in applied artificial intelligence. Inspired by places and cultures, she enjoys bringing people and different disciplines together to create something unexpected.

The story behind the atelier

Design with
a sense of place.

LIA ARMONÍA creates distinctive wedding worlds shaped by architecture, atmosphere and the people celebrating.

We believe the most beautiful weddings are personal, not predictable. Each project starts with curiosity about the setting, the couple and how they want their celebration to feel.

Our international approach brings together inspiring venues, local wedding planners and creative talents from around the world. We turn different perspectives into one thoughtful design direction.

Meet the founder. Diana Ponomarew is a designer and artist with an international business background, years of project management experience and a Master’s programme in Applied Artificial Intelligence. She loves discovering new places, connecting with people from different cultures and finding creative possibilities where others see ordinary spaces.

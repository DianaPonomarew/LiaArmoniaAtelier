LIA ARMONÍA is an international wedding design atelier. Founded by Diana Ponomarew, the studio approaches each celebration through the character of its setting.

We believe extraordinary design should feel like it belongs. Architecture, proportions, materiality and light shape every creative direction — rather than a formula repeated from one wedding to the next.

Working alongside local planners, venues and creative teams, the atelier develops visual concepts and thoughtful spatial direction for celebrations worldwide. On-site production and planning are delivered by independently engaged professionals.

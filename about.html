Diana Ponomarev · Founder & Creative Director

Where design thinking meets business strategy and technology.

LIA ARMONÍA is an international wedding design atelier founded by Diana Ponomarev. With extensive experience in international project management and a strong business mindset, she combines creative direction with an understanding of how ambitious ideas become cohesive realities.

Her background in intercultural management and ongoing Master's studies in Applied Artificial Intelligence inform a distinctive approach to design creation, architectural storytelling and visual innovation.

Connected to different countries, cultures and creative communities, Diana collaborates internationally with venues, wedding planners and production partners. The atelier focuses on artistic direction and spatial design while working alongside local specialists for on-site execution.

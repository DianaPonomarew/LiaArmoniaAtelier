At LIA ARMONÍA, we believe a wedding should feel like it belongs to its setting and to the people celebrating. Our work begins with listening, exploring and finding the details that make a place personal.

We connect couples with inspiring wedding venues, experienced local planners and creative partners around the world. Together, we shape an intentional design vision that can be carried through to the celebration itself.

Behind the atelier. Founder Diana Ponomarew is a designer and artist with a Master in Applied Artificial Intelligence and a background in international business and project management. She loves discovering extraordinary spaces and bringing people and ideas from different cultures together.

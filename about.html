01 / The studio

The art of creating atmosphere.

LIA ARMONÍA is an international wedding design atelier founded by Diana Ponomarew. We specialise in creative direction, spatial design and architectural storytelling for celebrations that feel unmistakably their own.

Our approach begins with understanding a place: its structure, scale, materials and light. From there, we develop a distinctive visual language that can inform ceremony design, the dining experience and the wider atmosphere of a wedding.

02 / Founder & Creative Director

Diana Ponomarew.

For Diana, the most meaningful design is not a formula to apply to every venue. It is a conversation between the character of a setting, the people celebrating and the emotion they want to create.

LIA ARMONÍA works internationally with couples, local planners, venues and creative collaborators. The atelier leads the visual direction; experienced on-site partners handle logistics, technical feasibility and event execution where separately engaged.

03 / How we work

From vision to direction.

Observe. We study the existing architecture, dimensions, context and practical parameters of a space.

Imagine. We develop a cohesive aesthetic through spatial concepts, imagery, materiality and considered details.

Translate. We prepare a clear direction for collaboration with planners and production professionals. Conceptual visualisations are distinguished from completed work and are subject to real-world feasibility.

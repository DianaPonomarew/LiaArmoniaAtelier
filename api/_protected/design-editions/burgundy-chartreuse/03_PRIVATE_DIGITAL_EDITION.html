01

The Ceremony Concept

Color becomes architecture.

The edition centers a coastal ceremony threshold where oxblood burgundy, acidic chartreuse, ivory stone and candlelight form a buildable ceremony composition. The venue architecture remains visible; the scenic wall, aisle rhythm, seating and floral gestures carry the atmosphere.

02

Design Language

What must stay legible.

Burgundy Structure

The wall and seating carry the deepest color. Substitutions should preserve the low, modern, upholstered and architectural feeling.

Chartreuse Tension

Florals introduce acidic green as a controlled interruption rather than an all-over theme.

Visible Stone

Negative space and neutral architecture are part of the design. Avoid overfilling the ceremony zone.

03

Ceremony Spatial Direction

A clear axis, a focal wall, and guest seating that frames the view.

Best suited to coastal, quarry, museum, hotel or private-estate architecture with vertical proportion, a level build zone, practical load-in and permission for freestanding scenic work.

04

Seating + Aisle

Use the chair as a production reference, not a blind retail order.

Seating Route

Burgundy upholstered ceremony chairs, low benches or modular joined seating can work if the silhouette remains restrained and monochrome.

Aisle Rhythm

Candlelight and floral clusters should support the aisle and focal zone only. Confirm accessibility, candle policy and wind protection early.

05

Scenic Architecture

The burgundy wall is the custom hero build.

Suitable routes include scenic flats with flexible skin, EPS foam with hardcoat, GRG or reinforced gypsum, or textile skin on a curved frame. Outdoor use requires venue-specific bracing, ballast and engineering review. No engineering approval is implied by this edition.

06

Color + Materials

Oxblood, chartreuse, ivory stone, candlelight.

White cylinder plinths, matte burgundy finish, warm ivory candle vessels and controlled floral color are the ceremony material priorities. Reception linen, tabletop, dinnerware, lounge and bar elements are intentionally outside this edition.

07

Sourcing + Fabrication

Quote targets, not endorsements.

Send the same concise brief to scenic fabricators, rental houses and floral teams: reference visuals, venue, indoor/outdoor status, desired dimensions, matte burgundy finish, install window, strike window, ballast needs and line-item pricing.

09

Production Handoff

Move from concept to ceremony through a clear sequence.

  1. Lock venue requirements: access, power, candle policy, floor protection and outdoor wind rules.
  2. Request quotes from rental, floral and scenic fabrication teams.
  3. Approve samples for burgundy finish, upholstery, candle vessels and floral color.
  4. Technicalize dimensions, engineering, ballast, stage details and lighting positions.
  5. Install and style the ceremony.
  6. Strike, return rentals and close vendor logistics.
10

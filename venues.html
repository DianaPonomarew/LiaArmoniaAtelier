08 / A FEW DETAILS

Good questions,
clear answers.

Can a venue use the visuals in its own marketing?

Yes, when a license is included in the written quote. We'll agree permitted channels, attribution, territories and duration before delivery. The underlying concepts and working files remain the studio's intellectual property unless expressly licensed or assigned.

Will LIA ARMONÍA recommend or feature our venue?

We may consider selected partner properties for relevant couple briefings or editorial features where there is genuine alignment and any required permission. This is not guaranteed and is independent of whether a concept package is purchased unless covered by a separate campaign agreement.

Do you provide full wedding planning or physical installation?

No. LIA ARMONÍA focuses on creative direction, spatial design and visualization. Local planners, venues and trusted production partners handle logistics, supplier booking, buildability and on-site execution.

What do you need from our team?

At minimum: venue name and location, the spaces to explore, usable photographs, approximate available dimensions or plans, desired visual direction, commercial usage goals, and target timing. We can start with a link to your property and advise on the exact materials needed.

AN OPEN, TRANSPARENT PROPOSAL

Made for
long-term trust.

15%
Proposed success-based referral commission

For a new couple whom LIA ARMONÍA personally introduces and who subsequently books your planning service, we propose a referral commission of 15% of the planning fees actually received by your studio.

This applies to your own planning-service fee only — not the couple's total wedding budget, venue hire, external vendor costs, taxes, or pass-through expenses. The exact scope, timing, and referral attribution would be agreed together in writing before any commission-bearing introduction.

No exclusivity. No upfront joining fee.

Being considered for the network does not guarantee referrals, require you to purchase design services or authorize use of your portfolio. Every project and any commission arrangement are subject to mutual agreement. We aim for clear, appropriate disclosure of referral arrangements.

More than referrals.

We are equally open to collaborative design commissions, creative production, visualizations for your clients, and longer-term partnerships tailored to your studio.

Atmosphere / Space

The Atelier

Wedding design
shaped by architecture,
atmosphere and
spatial experience.

Lia Armonía creates venue-specific wedding design through architecture, atmosphere, materiality and spatial direction. Each concept is developed in relation to the existing setting and visualised before production begins.

Discover our approach →
01Conceive

Understanding the venue, vision, narrative and emotional direction of the celebration.

+
Every commission begins with the setting itself: the architecture, proportions, light, landscape and the atmosphere the celebration should hold.
02Design

Translating architecture, atmosphere, materials, florals, furniture and light into one spatial concept.

+
The visual language is shaped as a complete world, from palette and texture to ceremony, dinner, paper, objects and movement.
03Realise

Turning the final creative direction into a clear visual framework for planners, vendors and production teams.

+
The result becomes a refined direction that can be understood, shared and produced with clarity before the wedding exists.

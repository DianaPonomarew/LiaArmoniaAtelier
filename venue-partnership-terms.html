2. Commission and excluded revenue

There is no listing or membership fee and no exclusivity. The standard new venue hire schedule is 10% of eligible net venue hire actually received and retained from a qualifying LIA introduction. A personal invitation may show an individual 5% schedule or another expressly agreed rate instead. Only the schedule displayed and accepted for that property applies. The individual offer is specific to the named property and is not transferable.

Net venue hire is the Partner’s own charge for use of the agreed event space, after discounts and refunds. It excludes VAT or sales tax, tourist taxes, service charges, gratuities, refundable security deposits, independent supplier charges, and separately priced accommodation, catering, food and beverage, equipment, planning and other services. Those other revenue categories are included only if expressly selected in an individual schedule.

The percentage is applied to the eligible revenue before any tax due on LIA’s own invoice. Applicable invoice tax, place of supply and any reverse charge treatment follow the law and the parties’ actual tax status; neither tax exemption nor automatic reverse charge is promised. The Partner supplies its accurate billing details and applicable VAT identification before invoicing.

3. Hospitality packages and minimum spends

If no separate venue hire charge is paid, a venue-hire-only schedule creates no commission on rooms or food and beverage. An individual hospitality schedule may instead expressly include registered referred group room revenue, a specified private event package, or a contracted private event food and beverage minimum. The displayed schedule identifies the permitted categories and the Partner selects the applicable ones before accepting.

For group accommodation, only rooms attributable to the registered referred group and project are eligible. For private gatherings, only the agreed contracted package or minimum spend actually received and retained is eligible; public restaurant sales, incidental guest spending and unrelated individual stays are excluded. All exclusions in section 2 continue to apply. A hire charge and a package component are never counted twice. Where a package mixes eligible and excluded items, the parties agree a reasonable allocation in writing before the introduction or any commission calculation. Additional services do not enter the commission base automatically.

4. Qualifying introductions and prior enquiries

LIA introduces the named client and event or project by email with a reference and introduction date, with the client’s permission to share the brief. The Partner acknowledges receipt. A qualifying booking is a new client contract arising from that introduction and signed within 12 months of it. There is no fee for a view, click, enquiry or introduction alone. Later instalments under that qualifying contract remain covered; unrelated future events, stays and repeat business do not.

An active documented prior enquiry about the same client and project should be reported within five business days with reasonable dated evidence. A mailing-list entry alone is insufficient. Genuine later evidence is considered fairly, and disputed overlap is resolved in writing before disputed commission is treated as due. A qualifying booking remains eligible if the client later books via another contact or agent, provided LIA’s introduction caused the booking. The Partner remains free to use other channels. Competing referral claims should be raised promptly for resolution, without an automatic obligation to pay duplicate commissions.

5. Reporting invoicing and instalments

The Partner reports a qualifying signed booking and each relevant payment, refund or cancellation within five business days. It provides the referral reference, contracted eligible categories, amounts, currency and payment dates reasonably needed to calculate commission. Proportionate redacted evidence may be requested. Banking access, unrelated contracts and full guest lists are not required.

Commission accrues proportionately only as the Partner receives and is entitled to retain money allocated to eligible revenue. Refundable security deposits and unpaid balances are excluded. LIA invoices the corresponding commission, payable within 14 calendar days after receipt of a valid invoice, never before the relevant client funds have been received. A good-faith dispute does not suspend payment of an undisputed portion. Invoices use the currency accepted in the schedule unless both parties agree a conversion method.

Example: at 10%, EUR 10,000 of eligible net hire produces EUR 1,000 commission if fully received and retained. A EUR 3,000 eligible instalment initially creates EUR 300 commission; the remaining EUR 700 accrues only on receipt of the remaining EUR 7,000. At 5%, the same EUR 3,000 instalment creates EUR 150 commission.

6. Cancellation refunds and postponement

No commission is due on unpaid or refunded eligible revenue. If commission has already been paid, LIA corrects the invoice and credits or returns the corresponding overpayment within 14 calendar days after the documented adjustment is agreed, unless both parties agree an offset against an imminent invoice. Eligible nonrefundable sums retained remain commissionable. A postponement is reconciled against the same project without charging twice. Refunds and replacement payments are counted once. Extra scope is eligible only within the accepted categories.

The complimentary profile is prepared from supplied property information, one main image and three to six gallery images. One consolidated factual correction round is included. The Partner approves the final presentation and permitted image use before public publication. Submission and commercial acceptance do not by themselves authorize immediate publication. Planned launch dates remain targets until separately confirmed.

Proposed promotion combines destination and experience discovery on LIA’s website, selected approved Instagram editorial and suitable direct introductions. Recommendations consider location, style, capacity, budget, availability and verified suitability. A higher commission does not buy a higher recommendation position. Paid or sponsored placement, if introduced, is identified separately. No minimum reach, posts, advertising spend, enquiries, bookings or revenue is guaranteed. Hosted stays, shoots, travel, new AI visuals, films and paid design services need a separate written scope and price or exchange agreement.

11. Duration suspension and termination

The partnership begins on express acceptance and continues until ended. Either party may terminate by email on 30 calendar days’ notice. LIA gives reasons for restriction or suspension before or at the time it takes effect and reasons on a durable medium at least 30 days before terminating the whole service, except where mandatory law permits an exception, such as a legal obligation or serious repeated breach. A reasonable opportunity to clarify or remedy is provided where required and practicable.

LIA removes its controlled public profile and ceases new promotional use within 10 business days after termination or an agreed earlier withdrawal. Rights or unlawful-permission concerns can be raised immediately. Copies outside LIA’s control cannot be recalled. Existing qualifying introductions retain their original 12 month signing window and fee rules; ending the partnership creates no rights over new projects. Private records are retained only as necessary for existing bookings, tax, legal or dispute purposes.

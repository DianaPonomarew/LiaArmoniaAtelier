This policy is a practical website notice for a California-based studio and should be reviewed by a qualified professional as the business grows.

Who we are

LIA ARMONÍA is a Wedding Design Atelier creating cinematic wedding worlds for couples, venues and wedding vendors. This policy explains how information submitted through liaarmonia.com, email, Instagram and related inquiry channels is handled.

Information collected

We may collect information you choose to send, including name, email address, phone number, project location, event timing, venue details, estimated investment, style direction, studio or vendor category, Instagram handle, collaboration details and message content.

How we use information

We use information to respond to inquiries, review project fit, prepare proposals, create private creative direction, manage communication and maintain reasonable business records.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share inquiry details with third parties for their own marketing. Limited information may be processed by service providers that help run the website, email, analytics, file storage, payment processing or creative production.

Cookies and analytics

The website or hosting platform may use basic cookies, analytics, spam protection or performance tools. Hosting, email and analytics providers may process technical information according to their own policies.

California privacy rights

California residents may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to legal and business record exceptions. We will not discriminate against you for exercising available privacy rights.

Retention

We keep inquiry and project information only as long as reasonably needed for communication, service delivery, record keeping, fraud prevention, dispute handling, tax or legal compliance.

Deletion requests

To request deletion, correction or a copy of information you submitted, email atelier@liaarmonia.com.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and platform-based safeguards, but no website, email system or hosting platform can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please do not send highly sensitive personal, medical, legal or financial documents unless specifically requested.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the website, studio services, tools or legal requirements change.

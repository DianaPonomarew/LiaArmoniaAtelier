Referral Partnership Terms

These terms govern qualifying wedding-planning referrals between the business operating under the name LIA ARMONÍA (“LIA”) and the independent professional wedding-planning studio identified in the Partner Portal (“Partner”). The Partner confirms that the person accepting these terms is authorized to bind the business.

1. Complimentary presentation

There is no fee to create or maintain an editorial studio profile, and no exclusivity. LIA may present the Partner’s services in its Wedding Planner Collection, subject to editorial and image permissions. Neither referrals nor bookings are guaranteed.

2. Qualifying referrals

A qualifying referral is a prospective client whose wedding-planning enquiry is transmitted or personally introduced to the Partner by LIA, identified by a referral reference or written notice, and who subsequently signs a new planning-services agreement with the Partner arising from that introduction. LIA will not disclose a prospective client’s identifying details without an appropriate basis and permission. If the Partner already has an active, documented relationship or enquiry with that same prospective client before LIA's referral notice, the Partner must notify LIA within five business days, with reasonable supporting information. The parties will resolve that overlap in writing before treating the introduction as commissionable.

3. Commission and payment

For each qualifying referral, the Partner owes LIA a referral commission of 15% of net wedding-planning service fees actually received from that client. “Net planning fees” means the Partner’s own planning and coordination service revenue excluding VAT/sales taxes, refunds, and payments made on behalf of the client to venues or independent third-party vendors. Commission accrues when each client payment is received, including deposits or instalments, and is payable within 14 calendar days after receipt of that client payment, against an invoice or payment instructions from LIA. The Partner shall notify LIA within five business days of each qualifying client booking, cancellation, refund or payment receipt, and provide the relevant received-fee amounts reasonably needed to calculate the commission.

4. Refunds and cancellation

No commission is due on fees never received. If commission was paid on a fee later refunded to the client, the corresponding commission will be credited against the next payable commission or refunded as agreed in writing. No commission applies to unrelated vendor revenue or any independent later project unless separately agreed.

5. Client relationship

The Partner independently sets its prices, contracts with the client, performs its services, and handles its own taxes and legal obligations. LIA does not guarantee clients, bookings, payment or service quality. Nothing creates an employment relationship, partnership, agency or exclusivity. The Partner must not present itself as an employee of LIA.

6. Privacy and marketing

Each party shall handle client and business contact data for the referral purpose in accordance with applicable data-protection law. The Partner must not publicly share confidential client details received through LIA without appropriate permission. Images and editorial materials are subject to the separate permissions recorded in the Partner Portal.

7. Duration and termination

Either party may end future participation by written notice to the other. Termination does not cancel commissions already accrued or commissions on qualifying introductions made before termination that lead to a booking within 12 months of the introduction, unless the parties expressly agree otherwise in writing. Changes to these terms require mutual agreement; later website updates alone do not amend already accepted terms.

8. Governing law and communications

The parties choose German law for this commercial agreement, subject to any overriding mandatory provisions that may apply. Any dispute shall be brought before the competent court under applicable jurisdiction rules. Notices may be sent by email to the addresses supplied during registration, and LIA may be contacted at atelier@liaarmonia.com.

9. Electronic acceptance

The Partner accepts these terms by deliberately selecting the separately labelled agreement checkbox and submitting the Partner Portal form. LIA records the contract version, acceptance timestamp, studio and authorized contact information in its private records. A copy of these terms should be retained by each party.

For partners: Please read the full terms before agreeing. If you have any questions, contact atelier@liaarmonia.com or book a complimentary partnership call.

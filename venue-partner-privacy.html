Legal bases

Contract steps and performance rely on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where the data subject is the contracting party. For a company’s representatives, necessary business communication, secure operation, evidence and legal claims rely on Article 6(1)(f), our legitimate interests in administering and protecting the relationship. Statutory record keeping relies on Article 6(1)(c). Separately optional publication permissions or marketing consent, where used, rely on the applicable permission and Article 6(1)(a) where consent is the relevant basis. Reading or acknowledging this notice is not marketing consent. You may withdraw consent for future processing without affecting lawful prior processing.

Contact details, the legal operator’s address, the acceptance record, private pricing and source files are not automatically published. Your team reviews the proposed feature first. Retention and deletion

Incomplete uploads are intended to be reviewed for deletion after 30 days; submissions not taken forward after 12 months. Active profile material is kept for the partnership, then removed from LIA-controlled public presentation within 10 business days after termination or an agreed earlier withdrawal. We keep only necessary private contract, invoice and dispute records for applicable statutory retention or limitation periods and any justified legal hold, then delete or anonymize them. If you request deletion sooner, we review any legal obligation or current claim that requires limited retention and explain the outcome. Retention reviews require administrative action; no automatic deletion timer is represented as operating.

Choices and your rights

You may contact us to exercise the rights above and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Portability and deletion apply within their legal limits. We may verify identity proportionately before releasing private files. We do not make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects; matching and publication are editorially reviewed.

Complaints and security

You may complain to a competent supervisory authority. For a private business in Bavaria, this includes the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA), lda.bayern.de. Submission files and agreement records are stored privately and private receipt/review links expire; keep them confidential. We apply access controls and limit data shared in notifications. No website or email service can guarantee absolute security.
